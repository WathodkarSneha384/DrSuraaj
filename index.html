Is there a doctor-led dietician and nutrition clinic in Wakad, Pune?

Yes. We offer personalised diet and nutrition plans designed alongside an MD physician, so your blood reports, medicines and conditions like diabetes or thyroid are considered in your plan.

What is IV nutritional therapy?

IV nutritional therapy delivers fluids, vitamins and minerals directly into the bloodstream through a drip. At our clinic every IV is prescribed only after a physician consultation and relevant tests, and is given under medical supervision.

Is IV therapy safe?

IV therapy is generally safe when it is medically indicated, prescribed by a qualified doctor and given in a clinical setting. It isn't suitable for everyone, which is why we assess your history, medicines and reports first.

Do you treat diabetes and help with weight loss?

Yes. Dr. Suraaj holds a Post Graduate Diploma in Clinical Endocrinology & Diabetes. We manage diabetes, pre-diabetes, blood pressure, cholesterol and weight with medication where needed plus nutrition and lifestyle plans.

Can I consult Dr. Suraaj online?

Yes. Private online video consultations start at ₹799. Book by phone or WhatsApp on +91 93717 10257.

When should I see a general physician?

See a general physician for fever, infections, cough and cold, stomach problems, body aches, and for ongoing conditions like blood pressure, diabetes and thyroid. A physician is also the right first stop for health check-ups and when you're unsure which specialist you need.
