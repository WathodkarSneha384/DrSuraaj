FAQs

Wellness questions, answered

Still unsure? Call or WhatsApp +91 93717 10257.

Where can I get IV therapy in Pune or Pimpri-Chinchwad?

Dr. Suraaj Clinic & Advanced Wellness Center in Wakad offers medically supervised IV therapy for patients from across Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad, including Hinjewadi, Baner, Pimple Saudagar, Ravet and Chinchwad. Every drip is prescribed by a registered physician after a consultation.

Is IV vitamin therapy safe?

IV therapy is generally safe when it is medically indicated, prescribed by a qualified doctor and given in a clinic with monitoring. It is not suitable for everyone, for example some people with heart or kidney disease, so we review your history, medicines and reports before recommending it.

Do I need a consultation before an IV drip?

Yes. We never give a drip without a physician consultation. If your reports show a deficiency or dehydration, the doctor will explain whether an IV, tablets or diet changes are the better option.

Can IV drips make my skin fairer?

We do not offer skin lightening. Our skin and hair drips support skin and hair health with nutrients and antioxidants, alongside diet and treatment of any underlying deficiency. Results vary from person to person.

What does a dietician consultation at the clinic include?

A review of your health, reports and routine, followed by a personalised diet plan that fits Indian meals and your schedule, with regular follow-ups. Because a physician is involved, plans also account for diabetes, thyroid, PCOS, cholesterol or kidney conditions.

How is medical weight loss different from a diet plan?

Medical weight management starts by checking for causes such as thyroid problems, insulin resistance or PCOS. The plan then combines nutrition, activity and, where appropriate, medicines prescribed by the doctor, with progress tracked over time.

Do you offer online diet and nutrition consultations?

Yes. Online consultations start at ₹799, so patients anywhere in Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad can start their nutrition plan from home. IV therapy needs a clinic visit.

What are the clinic timings?

The clinic is open all 7 days with morning and evening sessions, from 9 am and until 9:30 pm on most days. Timings can change on festivals, so please call +91 93717 10257 before visiting.
